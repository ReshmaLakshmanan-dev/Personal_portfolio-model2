Why I Choose Design

When Did I Switch to Design?

"After completing my 10th grade in 2018, I chose Computer Science as my stream for higher secondary education, as coding was gaining popularity. I completed my 12th grade in 2020 and pursued a B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) (Batch: 2020–2024), successfully graduating with first-class honors and no history of arrears. During my studies, I also completed the ADCP course, which covered C, C++, and Java. In my final year, I joined a Java Full-Stack internship, where I covered frontend technologies and discovered my passion for designing. This led me to self-learn UI/UX and graphic design through YouTube and online courses. After completing my degree, I worked for four months at a banking agency in Chennai, where I gained valuable experience in people’s mindsets, time management, and leadership skills. Within a short period, I was offered a promotion to Assistant Team Leader, but due to health issues, I had to return to my native place and quit my job to pursue freelancing. For the past three months, I have been working as a freelance graphic designer, specializing in UI/UX and visual design."
